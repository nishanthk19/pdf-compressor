Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We Have Answers.

How do I compress a PDF to an exact target MB size (e.g. 2MB or 500KB)? ▼

Simply select the Exact Size Compressor tool on PDF Precision, upload your document, and enter your target size in MB (or select a preset like 0.5 MB, 1.0 MB, or 2.0 MB). Our server iteratively adjusts stream compression levels to match your exact file size limit.

Are my uploaded PDF documents safe and private? ▼

Yes! Privacy is guaranteed. Every uploaded PDF file is processed in isolated server disk space and undergoes immediate automatic deletion (fs.unlinkSync) as soon as processing completes or on request termination. We never store, inspect, or share your documents.

How do I convert scanned PDF pages into selectable & searchable text? ▼

Select the OCR PDF tool, upload your scanned document or image-based PDF, and click Run OCR. The optical character recognition engine analyzes glyphs and overlays transparent text over the document, allowing you to highlight, copy, and search text seamlessly.

Is PDF Precision free to use without limits or account registration? ▼

Yes, PDF Precision is 100% free for all users. There are no registration forms, credit cards, or hidden watermarks added to your documents. Files up to 200MB are supported across all 8 processing tools.
